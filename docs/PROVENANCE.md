# Project provenance

BetterButtons was rebuilt from the maintainer's earlier private MegaButtons prototype and renamed before its planned open-source launch. The repository contains no runtime dependencies, remote fonts, stock imagery or bundled third-party design assets.

AI-assisted development tools, including Gemini and Codex, were used during implementation and review. Every contribution still needs human review for correctness, licensing and originality; tool output is not treated as proof of ownership.

## Maintainer attestation

Before changing repository visibility or publishing a package, the maintainer must confirm that:

- they have the right to license all code, copy, icons and visual assets under MIT;
- no proprietary client, employer or third-party material was copied into the project;
- any future third-party asset includes its license and required attribution;
- the BetterButtons name and branding are acceptable for public use;
- the commit selected for release contains no credentials or private data.

If provenance cannot be established for a file, replace or remove it before public release rather than relying on assumption.
