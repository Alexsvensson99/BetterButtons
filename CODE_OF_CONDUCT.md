# Code of Conduct

BetterButtons is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting corrections.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's personal information.
- Make room for newcomers and explain project-specific context.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, repeated disruption, doxxing and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not accepted in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method on [Alexander Svensson's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Alexsvensson99). Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban a participant. Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as reasonably possible. Anyone involved in a report will step aside from deciding its outcome where a conflict of interest exists.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents BetterButtons. Honest technical disagreement is welcome when expressed constructively.
